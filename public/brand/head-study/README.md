# Reporta head study

Six exploratory vector expressions. The canonical logo is unchanged.
Each variant includes a transparent head SVG/PNG and light/dark full logo SVG/PNG.
Lettering, kerning and body paths are identical to the master. The new elongated
head overlaps the final body segment to remove the abrupt round-head junction.
Red #FF462A, white #FFFFFF and cyan #00B8EC are unchanged across themes.
